# Public dashboard demonstration (3–5 minutes)

1. Open **Overview**. Identify the synthetic-demo and read-only states, leading diagnosis, governed outcome, and validated lifecycle.
2. Open **Detection** to inspect the source-bound metric visual and its explicit data-quality context.
3. In **Investigation**, compare ranked hypotheses and expand their evidence/falsifier details; note that probabilities are deterministic.
4. Use **Evidence** to trace the sanitized artifact trail.
5. Open **Impact** and distinguish validated synthetic estimation context from a production claim.
6. In **Remediation & Recovery**, inspect the historical policy, approval requirement, simulated execution receipt, and recovery test outcomes. No button executes anything.
7. In **Evaluation**, review calibration, coverage, abstention and zero authority-violation measures.
8. Finish in **System & Limitations** to review the static architecture and explicit authority boundary.
